Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the privacy practices applicable to Checklist ("the App"). We are committed to respecting your privacy, protecting your personal data, and providing a clean, accessible, and free alternative to existing applications.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, access, or process personal information for advertising, profiling, behavioral tracking, or other unrelated commercial purposes. We do not seek to collect sensitive personal information through the App.

Where account registration and authentication are required, limited information, such as your login email address and account credentials or authentication identifiers, may be processed to create, authenticate, and maintain your account. Information you create or store through the App may also be processed to provide the functionality you request.

We do not intentionally collect personal information beyond what is necessary to operate the App and provide its core features. We do not sell personal information.

2. How We Use Information

We do not use users' personal information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or commercial exploitation. Information processed by the App is used only as necessary to provide its intended functionality, authenticate users, maintain accounts, store and synchronize user-created data, maintain service security, and respond to user requests.

Our objective is to provide a clean, straightforward, and free alternative to existing applications without intrusive advertising or unnecessary use of personal information.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties for their own advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

Information is not disclosed to third parties except where necessary to operate and secure the App, to comply with applicable legal obligations, or where disclosure is otherwise lawfully required. Service providers may process information on our behalf where necessary to host, authenticate, or maintain the App.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may delete their data directly through the App. When the deletion process is completed, the associated user data stored on our servers will be deleted, subject to any retention required by applicable law or necessary for legitimate security and operational purposes.

If you wish to request the deletion of personal data associated with your account, you may contact us using the email address provided in the Contact Us section. Please contact us from the email address associated with your account so that we can verify your request.

We will process verified deletion requests in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Any information that must be retained for legal reasons will be retained only for the required period and handled accordingly.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

Access to account-related information is limited to what is reasonably necessary to operate and maintain the App. Although we take reasonable steps to safeguard information, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

We respect the privacy of all users, regardless of age or gender. We do not discriminate between users on the basis of these characteristics in our privacy practices.

Where personal information is processed to provide the App's functionality, it is handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws. Additional legal protections may apply to children depending on their age and jurisdiction.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, as well as other rights available under applicable data protection legislation.

You may delete your data through the App or contact us to submit a privacy-related request. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.
