Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Checklist handles information when you use our application.

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Checklist is designed to provide a clean, straightforward checklist experience. We do not collect personal information for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

To provide core app functionality, we may process the following information:

  • Account information: Your email address and the authentication information needed to create, secure, and access your account.
  • User-created content: Checklists, items, and related information you choose to save in the app.

This information may be stored on our servers to support account access and app functionality.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only as needed to operate and maintain Checklist, including to:

  • Create and authenticate user accounts.
  • Save and manage your checklists and items.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the app.
  • Respond to support requests and account deletion requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, or user profiling. Our goal is to provide a clean and free alternative to existing checklist applications.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for their advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the app, such as providers that host our servers or support authentication. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

Our use of service providers depends on the services actually used by the app and its backend.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your data through the deletion functionality available in the app, where supported. This removes the associated data from our servers according to the app's deletion process.

If you want to delete your account and associated personal data, you can also contact us using the email address associated with your account. We may verify your identity before processing the request.

To request account deletion, visit our Account Deletion page.

Some information may be retained when required by law or when reasonably necessary for legitimate security purposes.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

We aim to treat all users' information respectfully and consistently, regardless of age or gender. This does not mean that the app is intended for every age group.

If the app is used by children, any applicable age-related privacy requirements will be respected. If you believe information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. You can contact us to ask about these rights or submit a request.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our app, practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
